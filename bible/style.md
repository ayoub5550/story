# Style Bible — الدليل البصري (canonical)

## Look
Hand-painted **2D animated feature** look: clean expressive ink lines, cel-shaded characters, painterly gouache backgrounds, subtle film grain. Mythic, warm, unhurried. Think classic hand-drawn epic animation crossed with **Saharan rock art** (Tassili / Ahaggar engravings).
Never photoreal, never 3D-plastic/CGI look, never anime chibi.

## STYLE PREFIX (prepend to EVERY image/video prompt — do not paraphrase)
```
Hand-painted 2D animated feature film, clean expressive ink linework, cel-shaded characters, painterly gouache backgrounds, warm Saharan palette (ochre, burnt sienna, sand gold, deep indigo, night ultramarine), subtle film grain, cinematic 16:9 composition, no text, no subtitles, no watermark.
```

## NEGATIVE (append where the model supports it, or as "Avoid: ...")
```
photorealistic, 3D render, CGI plastic, anime chibi, modern objects, guns, cars, horses, mosques, Arabic or Latin text, logos, deformed hands, visible mouth on veiled men, comic panels, split screen, collage, multiple frames, storyboard grid
```

## Palette by world-state
| State | Scenes | Palette / light |
|---|---|---|
| Frame story (today) | S01, S18 | Firelight orange + deep night blue, stars, warm close faces |
| Soft-stone era (myth) | S02 | Pastel, glowing, stone looks like wet clay, slightly surreal, dreamy bloom |
| Part 1 — clarity era | S03–S09 | Bright ochre days, golden hours; night = indigo + silver moon |
| Part 2 — hard stone | S10–S17 | More contrast, colder shadows, basalt greys and rust; hope returns as gold in S16–S17 |
| Kel Essuf / spirits | S05 | Desaturated, translucent white shapes in heat haze |

## Camera grammar
- Wide establishing shots are the emotional language (vast desert, tiny humans). Average shot 6–9 s.
- Slow moves only: slow push-in, slow pan, crane up. Fast cuts only in raid (S10) and escape (S13/S15).
- Conflict staging (Part 1): AMAMELLEN on screen-left looking right, ADELASEGH on screen-right looking left. After reconciliation (S08) they sit **side by side facing the same direction**.
- Lip-sync avoidance (key production rule): men are always veiled; unveiled women speak in profile, back-to-camera, wide shots or V.O.

## Recurring visual motifs
1. **Footprints in stone** (soft era) → **no footprints** (hard era).
2. **The dot game**: dots + circles + lines drawn in sand — becomes Tifinagh.
3. **Hands on stone**: S02 giant hand, S08 reconciliation, S14 carving, S18 child's hand.
4. **Indigo vs sky-indigo** veils = uncle vs nephew.

## Tifinagh
Use real Tifinagh glyphs (e.g. ⵣ ⵎ ⵏ ⵜ ⴰ ⵙ ⵔ ⵍ ⴷ ⵖ, dots and circles of Tuareg tifinagh) for any inscription. Generative models garble glyphs → glyph close-ups are built as **HyperFrames/overlay layers** (crisp vector glyphs composited on painted rock), never trusted to the video model.

## Sound
- Narration: GRANDMOTHER (ElevenLabs `eleven_v4`, language_code `ar`).
- Music identity: **imzad** (one-string fiddle) drone + **tende** drum + women's ululation at climaxes; tonal centre D minor/Dorian. Music is NOT generated by the video model.
- Ambience: wind, sand hiss, camels, fire crackle, distant jackals at night.
- Target loudness: −16 LUFS integrated for the final mix, dialogue at −20 LUFS.
